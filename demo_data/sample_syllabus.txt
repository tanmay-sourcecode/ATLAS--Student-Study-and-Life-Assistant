CS 3110: Advanced Programming
Fall Semester 2026 - Syllabus

Course Description:
CS 3110 is the third course in the programming sequence for computer science majors.
It covers functional programming concepts, compiler designs, data structures, and multi-threaded systems.
The language of instruction is OCaml.

Grading Policy:
- Assignments: 40% (4 major coding projects)
- Midterm Exam: 25% (In-class, covers modules 1 to 5)
- Final Exam: 30% (Comprehensive)
- Participation & Quizzes: 5%

Academic Integrity:
All work submitted must be your own. Collaborations are limited to high-level conceptual discussions.
Plagiarism, code sharing, or posting solutions online is strictly prohibited and will result in a zero grade.
