# Skill: study_plan
Given the student's deadlines, upcoming exams, classes, and available study hours, create a balanced study plan.
Optimize for workload realism and deadline risk.
Output a day-by-day study schedule containing:
1. Clear dates/days leading up to the target exam.
2. Specific topics to review on each day.
3. Suggested practice quiz blocks or review questions.
4. Short rationale explaining why this pacing is chosen.
Keep output highly structured and realistic (no 12-hour study recommendations).
