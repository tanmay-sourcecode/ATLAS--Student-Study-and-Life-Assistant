# Skill: deadline_checkin
Review all upcoming deadlines and current status of tasks.
Your checkin output must:
1. List upcoming deadlines sorted by urgency.
2. Flag any high-priority tasks that are still in 'Todo' status and have a deadline in the next 48 hours as "At Risk".
3. Recommend a single, immediate next action (e.g. 'Start CS 3110 Assignment 3 focus block').
Keep it concise, friendly, and action-oriented.
