# Skill: document_summary
Summarize the ingested lecture slides, syllabus, or notes.
Your summary must contain:
1. High-level Overview (3-4 sentences of the document's main focus).
2. Key Definitions and terms.
3. Top 3 likely exam/test topics with a short explanation of why they are important.
4. 5 review questions the student can use to test their knowledge.
Ground everything strictly in the retrieved text and cite the filename/page.
